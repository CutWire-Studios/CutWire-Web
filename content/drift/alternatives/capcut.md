CapCut is the editor most people mean when they say “make a Reel tonight.” It is fast. It is also a product with a **free watermark**, an **account**, and features that move behind Pro.

**Drift** copies the part worth copying — a timeline that does not fight you, effects, stickers, captions, cutouts — and drops the strings. You install an app on your computer or Android phone, drop in clips, and export an MP4 that does not advertise someone else’s brand.

![Drift voiceover dialog with local text-to-speech and voice cloning](/images/drift-voiceover.avif)

## Who should switch

- You hit a watermark on a video you wanted to post
- You want a **CapCut alternative on Linux**
- You do not want footage sitting in someone else’s cloud
- You want captions, **text-to-speech with voice cloning** and cutouts that run locally after a free model download
- You want a native app with a bigger multi-track timeline, keyframe graph editor and tracking

## Who should stay on CapCut

If you live inside CapCut’s template store, or you edit on iPhone, stay for now. Drift is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.cutwire.drift) for Android, but there is no iOS app. Drift does not pretend to replace CapCut’s template catalog.

## What you get in Drift instead

A multi-track timeline, GPU effects and 23 effect templates, local auto captions with edit-by-transcript, subject and people cutouts, beat tools, color grading with scopes, 3D and export that matches preview. On Android you get the touch layout; grading, the graph editor and tracking are desktop only. Details live on the [feature list](/drift/features).
