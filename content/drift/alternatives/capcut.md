CapCut is the editor most people mean when they say “make a Reel tonight.” It is fast. It is also a product with a **free watermark**, an **account**, and features that move behind Pro.

**Drift** copies the part worth copying — a timeline that does not fight you, effects, stickers, captions, cutouts — and drops the strings. You download a desktop app, drop in clips, and export an MP4 that does not advertise someone else’s brand.

## Who should switch

- You hit a watermark on a video you wanted to post
- You want a **CapCut alternative on Linux**
- You do not want footage sitting in someone else’s cloud
- You prefer a native desktop window over a phone-sized UI

## Who should stay on CapCut

If you live inside CapCut’s template store, or you edit on iPhone, stay for now. Drift has an [Android APK](/drift#download) you sideload — not a Play Store app — and there is no iOS app. Drift does not pretend to replace CapCut’s template catalog.

## What you get in Drift instead

A multi-track timeline, GPU looks, auto captions on your machine, subject cutouts, beat tools and export that matches preview. Details live on the [feature list](/drift/features).
