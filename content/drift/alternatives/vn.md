VN and InShot own a simple job: **edit a TikTok on the device that filmed it**. They are not villains. Watermarks, Pro locks and tiny screens show up the moment the video matters.

**Drift** runs on **Android** and on **Linux, Windows and macOS**. It has the same social intent on both (captions, stickers, vertical presets, beat tools, voiceover), files on your own storage and no watermark. Edit on your phone, or move the clip to a computer for a bigger timeline plus grading, tracking and the keyframe graph editor. Drift keeps portrait orientation either way.

![Drift captions panel with timed cues](/images/drift-captions.avif)

## Who should switch

- You searched for a **VN alternative** or **InShot alternative**
- The watermark is embarrassing on a client video
- You want one editor on Android and on PC or Linux
- You want local captions and text-to-speech instead of gated in-app ones
- You want a mouse, a multi-track timeline and a proper keyboard when you are at a computer

## Who should stay on VN or InShot

Stay if you edit on iPhone. Drift has no iOS app. Stay too if you want VN's phone-first gesture polish and templates. Drift is on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.cutwire.drift) for Android, and APKs are on GitHub. The phone layout has no full color grading, graph editor or tracking panel.
