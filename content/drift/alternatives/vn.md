VN and InShot own a simple job: **edit a TikTok on the device that filmed it**. They are not villains. They are also not a desktop NLE. Watermarks, Pro locks and tiny screens show up the moment the video matters.

**Drift** is the **desktop alternative to VN and InShot**: same social intent (captions, stickers, vertical presets, beat tools), bigger timeline, files on your disk, no watermark. Import the phone clip — Drift keeps portrait orientation.

## Who should switch

- You searched for a **VN alternative** or **InShot alternative** on PC or Linux
- The watermark is embarrassing on a client video
- You want a mouse, a multi-track timeline and a proper keyboard

## Who should stay on VN or InShot

Stay for edits you will only ever make on a phone today. An [Android build of Drift](https://github.com/CutWire-Studios/Drift-Android) is under development; there is no iOS app yet.
