Kdenlive is what you should use if you wanted **Premiere, but free**. Many “Kdenlive alternative” searches are the opposite: people who do **not** want Premiere.

**Drift** is that other FOSS lane — CapCut-shaped, still GPLv3, still native. It also has docking workspaces (Edit, Color, Keyframes, Masking and Tracking and more), multicam, scopes, a keyframe graph and **3D with a scene camera**. Karaoke titles and look templates are not an afterthought.

![Drift 3D inspector with model and camera controls](/images/drift-3d.avif)

## Who should switch

- You teach, make Shorts, or edit game clips and Kdenlive feels heavy
- You want Whisper captions and local text-to-speech without assembling a plugin stack
- You want 3D layers, gaussian splats or a camera move inside a normal edit
- You like KDE software but not for this job

## Who should stay on Kdenlive

Stay for documentaries, proxy 4K, or a layout you already customized. Kdenlive has the longer track record. Point beginners at Drift.
