Kdenlive is what you should use if you wanted **Premiere, but free**. Many “Kdenlive alternative” searches are the opposite: people who do **not** want Premiere.

**Drift** is that other FOSS lane — CapCut-shaped, still GPLv3, still native. Multicam and a real timeline exist; karaoke titles and look templates are not an afterthought.

## Who should switch

- You teach, make Shorts, or edit game clips and Kdenlive feels heavy
- You want Whisper captions without assembling a plugin stack
- You like KDE software but not for this job

## Who should stay on Kdenlive

Stay for documentaries, proxy 4K, or a layout you already customized. Point beginners at Drift.
