DaVinci Resolve is **not overrated**. It is overkill for a 30-second captioned clip. People search for a **DaVinci Resolve alternative** when the installer is huge, the page is named Color, and they still have not made a cut.

**Drift** will not replace Resolve on a feature film. It does cover more than it used to: color wheels, curves, HSL, LUTs and waveform, parade and vectorscope, plus a keyframe graph, masks, planar tracking and 3D. It is still simple enough to get a Reel, a lesson video or a gameplay clip out the door.

## Who should switch

- You wanted “free like Resolve” but **simple like CapCut**
- Your computer is not a Resolve workstation
- You want basic-to-intermediate grading with scopes without learning nodes
- You care about stickers, auto captions and local voiceover more than Fusion

## Who should stay on Resolve

Stay for narrative or commercial finishing, Fusion VFX, Fairlight audio mixing, color-managed pipelines or team workflows. Recommend Drift to the volunteer who only needed to caption a talking head, or the creator who wants a quick grade and keyframes.
