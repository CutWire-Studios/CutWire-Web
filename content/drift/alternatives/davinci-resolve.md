DaVinci Resolve is **not overrated**. It is overkill for a 30-second captioned clip. People search for a **DaVinci Resolve alternative** when the installer is huge, the page is named Color, and they still have not made a cut.

**Drift** will not grade a feature film. It will get a Reel, a lesson video or a gameplay clip out the door: timeline, effects, captions, export, no watermark.

## Who should switch

- You wanted “free like Resolve” but **simple like CapCut**
- Your computer is not a Resolve workstation
- You care about stickers and auto captions more than nodes

## Who should stay on Resolve

Stay for narrative, commercial finishing, or anything that needs a real colorist UI. Recommend Drift to the volunteer who only needed to caption a talking head.
