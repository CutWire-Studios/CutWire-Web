**CutWire Drift** is a free, open-source video editor for Linux, Windows, macOS and Android. People usually find it after they search for a **CapCut alternative**, a **free video editor with no watermark**, or a replacement for the app they already use.

Drift has a multi-track timeline, local auto captions and edit-by-transcript, local text-to-speech with voice cloning, color grading with scopes, a keyframe graph editor, masking and motion tracking, 3D with gaussian splats, and subject cutouts. AI features run locally after a free model download. Grading, the graph editor and tracking are desktop only.

Pick the tool you know. Each page is an honest comparison — including where the other app still wins.

Open-source CapCut-style clones (browser or one-click installers) show up in the same searches. Drift is a **native Qt + FFmpeg app**, GPLv3, with a real timeline, local AI and export that matches preview. Use the table pages below rather than a thin list of clone names.
