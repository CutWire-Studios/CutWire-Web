Shotcut is the default answer to “**open source video editor**.” It earns that. If you live in filters and unusual codecs, stay.

**Drift** exists because a lot of people who type that query actually wanted **CapCut without the watermark**. Stickers, karaoke captions, beat drop looks, subject cutouts — still GPLv3, still FFmpeg under the hood. It has also grown tools Shotcut does not emphasize: 3D layers and a scene camera, a keyframe graph editor, motion tracking and a Source Lab for prepping clips.

![Drift 3D inspector with model and camera controls](/images/drift-3d.avif)

## Who should switch

- You bounced off Shotcut’s density
- You want on-device speech captions, local text-to-speech and a sticker panel
- You want 3D, tracking or keyframe curves in the same editor
- Linux + social video is the job, not broadcast mastering

## Who should stay on Shotcut

Stay for weird formats, hardware encode rabbit holes, or production you already trust to Shotcut. Drift is younger (pre-1.0) and Shotcut’s filter breadth and years of testing still go further.
