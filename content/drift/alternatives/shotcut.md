Shotcut is the default answer to “**open source video editor**.” It earns that. If you live in filters and unusual codecs, stay.

**Drift** exists because a lot of people who type that query actually wanted **CapCut without the watermark**. Stickers, karaoke captions, beat drop looks, subject cutouts — still GPLv3, still FFmpeg under the hood, different UI bet.

## Who should switch

- You bounced off Shotcut’s density
- You want on-device speech captions and a sticker panel
- Linux + social video is the job, not broadcast mastering

## Who should stay on Shotcut

Stay for weird formats, hardware encode rabbit holes, or production you already trust to Shotcut. Drift is younger.
