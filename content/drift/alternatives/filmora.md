Filmora sells a familiar story: “easy editor, lots of effects.” The plot twist is the **watermark** and the **license**. If you wanted Filmora because it looked like CapCut on a PC, you do not need to pay Wondershare to finish a school project.

**Drift** is that consumer-friendly timeline as GPLv3 software. Effects, transitions, karaoke titles, captions, cutouts, grading with scopes, keyframes and tracking — export without a logo in the corner.

![Drift Color panel with wheels and curves](/images/drift-color.avif)

## Who should switch

- You searched for a **free Filmora alternative** or “Filmora no watermark”
- You want Linux
- You want grading with scopes, a keyframe graph and motion tracking without a paid tier
- You are done with trial countdowns

## Who should stay on Filmora

Stay if you already paid and the stock library is the reason you open it. Drift will not match that store.
